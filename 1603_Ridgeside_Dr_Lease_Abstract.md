**Common features across all four leases:**
- None of the leases have a **separately itemized management fee** — in each case, the "administrative fee" (a flat **15% markup on total CAM/common-area operating costs**) is the only management-fee-like charge, embedded inside the CAM definition (Article 10.6(b) of the standard form).
- No lease specifies a **CAM/OpEx expense cap**, **base year**, or **tenant audit right**.
- Rent escalations across all four leases are **fixed-schedule** (either flat 3% compounding or fixed 2% compounding on base rent) — **none are CPI-indexed**.
- Security deposits: Rita's $2,000 / Reflections $2,000 / Brett Elmore $2,000 / MK Wellness $1,428 (all non-interest-bearing, commingle permitted).
- Personal guaranties: required for Reflections (3 individual guarantors) and originally contemplated for MK Wellness (1 guarantor, Mahrukh Kahn); explicitly waived/deleted for Rita's and Brett Elmore (both individual tenants already personally liable).

## 4. Key Flags for Reconciliation

1. **Landlord entity transition undocumented.** Leases originate with "John Barnes" or "John S. Barnes" individually; more recent addenda (Reflections Addendum #4, 2025; Brett Elmore Addendum #1, 2026) are signed by "Darren Barnes/Darren M. Barnes, President" of Barnes, Inc. No deed, assignment, or corporate formation document is included in any file. **Confirm current fee owner and lease-counterparty entity before underwriting.**
2. **Brett Elmore space appears to have been split.** The original 2017 lease demised **Units 3 and 4 together (2,448 SF)**, with Tenant's Proportionate Share fixed at 50%. The 2026 addendum (labeled "Addendum #1" but described in its own text as "a third addendum") covers only **1,224 SF ("Suite 3")** — implying Units 3 and 4 were split into two separate leasable suites at some point between 2017 and 2026, with Suite 4's current leasing status unknown (not provided in this document set — may be vacant, occupied by another tenant not included here, or reabsorbed into common area). **Recommend confirming Suite 4's current status and whether the 50% proportionate-share figure in the original lease was ever formally updated.**
3. **Proportionate-share percentages do not cleanly reconcile to one consistent building total.** Rita's (25%), Reflections (25%), and Brett Elmore's original lease (50%) foot exactly to a 4,896 SF building (Suite 1 + Suite 2 + Units 3&4). MK Wellness's lease also states a fixed 25% share on its 1,224 SF, which would require a building total of ~4,896 SF that has no room left for MK Wellness's own suite — meaning either MK Wellness's Suites 5 & 6 sit in a separate building/parcel with its own 25% calculation, or the 25% figures across leases were copied from a template without updating for the actual full-property square footage. **Recommend obtaining a certified rent roll / site plan to confirm true total GLA and correct pro-rata percentages for all tenants.**
4. **MK Wellness Lease, page 1 handwritten note.** A marginal note lists monthly rent figures ($1,851.30 / $1,953.30 / $2,055.30) that do not match the signed, typed Section 3.1 rent schedule ($1,428 / $1,530 / $1,632/month for Years 1–3). The typed schedule is the operative, signed rate; the handwritten note appears to be an unincorporated broker/landlord working figure and should be reconciled against actual billing history.
5. **MK Wellness Lease recital date inconsistency.** The lease's opening "WITNESSETH" recital references an effective date of "January 1st, 2023," while the operative commencement date stated in Section 2.1 and used throughout the rent schedule is **January 1, 2024**. Likely a template drafting carryover; commencement is treated as 1/1/2024 for purposes of this abstract.
6. **Reflections Addendum #2 (2015 term) is unsigned by Tenant** — the Tenant signature block is blank on that specific addendum, though it was signed by Landlord and all three guarantors. The rent schedule set by that addendum was carried forward and paid under subsequent addenda, suggesting it was honored in practice, but its formal execution status should be confirmed.
7. **Reflections — informal side letter.** An August 11, 2020 letter (Donald Nichols to John Barnes) purports to grant Reflections an early-termination-for-business-closure right (30 days' notice) tied to Addendum #3, but this was never incorporated into the formal lease/addendum language. Its enforceability — especially against a successor landlord — is uncertain and should be reviewed by counsel.
8. **Reflections — unsigned tenant "Attachment."** An undated attachment proposes several modifications (tax bill copies, disputes personal-guaranty enforceability, flags a CAM rate discrepancy of $1.25 vs. an alleged $0.50/SF). Its signature blocks are blank; unclear whether it was ever formally incorporated into the lease.
9. **Rita's — franchise collateral assignment.** Exhibit D to the Rita's lease conditionally assigns the lease to Rita's Water Ice Franchise Company, LLC for collateral/financing purposes only, with Landlord's consent to Franchisor step-in rights on tenant default. Not an operational concern today but relevant to any assignment/estoppel analysis.
10. **Several rent-schedule cells across Rita's Exhibit A/Addendum #1 and Brett Elmore's Exhibit A are handwritten/overwritten** and partially illegible (marked with ~ or "TBD" in the detailed sections below); clean copies should be obtained if precise historical billing reconciliation is required.

## 6. Summary Observations for Underwriting

- **In-place blended rent:** ~$102.6K/yr combined base rent + CAM (fixed components only) across the four suites (1,224 SF each), excluding variable tax/insurance passthroughs on three of the four leases — call it **~$21–26/SF fully-loaded** per suite once taxes/insurance are added, consistent across tenants.
- **Escalation risk is low/predictable:** all four leases use fixed-schedule increases (2–3% or stepped $/SF), not CPI, so near-term NOI growth from these tenants is contractually known through at least 2027–2028 for three of the four (Reflections' schedule is set only through Year 2 of its current term, i.e., through 8/31/26, with Years 3–5 CAM/tax/insurance marked TBD in the executed document itself).
- **Rollover exposure:** Brett Elmore's current term is short (expires 12/31/2027, only 18 months out) with no clearly documented further option — this is the nearest-term rollover risk in the rent roll. Rita's rolls in 2029, Reflections in 2030, MK Wellness in 2029 (all with at least one further option, except Reflections).
- **Diligence items before finalizing underwriting:** resolve Flags #1–#10 above, particularly (a) confirming current legal landlord entity, (b) confirming Suite 4's status and whether it is separately leased or vacant, and (c) obtaining a certified rent roll/site plan to true up the proportionate-share percentages across all tenants.
